# Room — a fintech product concept

Two working prototypes answering Canada's hardest everyday money question: **TFSA, RRSP, or FHSA?**

- `index.html` — case-study landing page
- `contribution-optimizer.html` — explainable allocation tool (2026 CRA limits, per-province marginal rates)
- `noa-scanner.html` — simulated AI extraction of contribution room from a CRA Notice of Assessment, with confidence scores and human-in-the-loop review

Pure static HTML, no build step, no dependencies. Keep all three files in the same directory — they cross-link.

Built by Mohammed Omer Shaik as a product management case study. Independent concept; not affiliated with any financial institution. Not financial or tax advice.
